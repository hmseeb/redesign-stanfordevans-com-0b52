Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

We offer pressure washing, window washing, address painting (including Texas flags and university logos), junk removal, leaf pickup, and gutter cleaning across the Dallas–Fort Worth area.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the job. Pressure washing typically runs $100–$500, window washing $150–$350, gutter cleaning $100–$250, and junk removal $100–$800 depending on volume. We always give you a free quote before starting — no surprises.

What areas do you serve?

We cover Dallas, Richardson, Plano, Frisco, Allen, McKinney, Garland, Addison, Carrollton, Coppell, Lewisville, Flower Mound, Highland Park, University Park, and 15+ more cities across DFW.

How does the 20% referral offer work?
