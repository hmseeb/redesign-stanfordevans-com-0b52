Privacy Policy

How Stanford Evans Property Services handles the information you share with us.

Overview

Stanford Evans Property Services is a student-run property services company serving the Dallas–Fort Worth area. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us about pressure washing, window washing, address painting, junk removal, leaf pickup, or gutter cleaning, and what we do with it.

Information We Collect

We only collect what we need in order to quote and complete work you ask us about:

  • Your name and the contact details you give us, such as a phone number or email address.
  • The service address or neighborhood where the work would take place.
  • Details about the job itself — the service you want, surfaces or areas involved, and timing.
  • Anything else you choose to include in a call, text, or quote request.

How We Use It

  • To prepare and send you a free quote.
  • To reach you about scheduling, arrival times, and follow-up on a job.
  • To apply our 20% referral discount when you or a neighbor mention a referral.
  • To keep basic records of the work we have performed.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We contact you about the work you asked us about — not with unrelated sales calls.

Calls and Text Messages

Our phone number, (214) 404-3931, accepts both calls and text messages. If you contact us by text, we will reply by text about your quote or job. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time and we will.

Quote Requests

Our online quote form is hosted at stanfordevans.com/quote-form. Information you submit there reaches us so a real person can follow up with pricing. The form is operated through a third-party website platform, which processes the submission on our behalf and is subject to its own terms.

Data Retention and Security

We keep quote requests and job records only as long as they are useful for serving you and keeping our own business records. We take reasonable care to limit access to your information to the people on our crew who need it to do the work.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are offered to property owners and residents. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Choices

You can ask us what information we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just call or text (214) 404-3931 and let us know.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page. Continuing to contact us or use our services after an update means the current version applies.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Call or text (214) 404-3931, or send us a note through our quote form.
